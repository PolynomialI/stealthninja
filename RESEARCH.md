# RESEARCH.md: reference inventory for the stealth assassin game

Reference game: *Stealth Master: Assassin Ninja* (SayGames LTD, 2020). iOS App Store id 1518389230;
Android package `com.stealthgame.master`. Latest builds seen: 1.12.36 (APKMirror) and 1.12.45 (APK sites).
Google Play: 4.2–4.4★, 100M+ installs.

This list covers **gameplay mechanics only**. Names, characters, art, sounds and level layouts in our game will be
our own. That includes the original's pop-culture parody heroes (see §7), which we won't imitate.

## How this was gathered (read first)
- This session's network blocks the store pages and every fan or review site (apps.apple.com, play.google.com,
  levelwinner.com, zoomg.ir, bluestacks.com all refused). **Every fact below comes from web-search result summaries
  of those pages, not from reading the pages myself.** Wording is paraphrased unless shown in quotes, and quotes are
  as the search engine reported them.
- No fan wiki, no video transcripts and no level-by-level walkthrough turned up. Search found no YouTube gameplay
  videos.
- **Confirmed** = official store listing text (App Store / Google Play / APKMirror's copy of the store text).
  **Uncertain** = only third-party sources (guides, reviews, APK or browser-game sites), or no source at all.
  Some third-party "Stealth Master" pages describe a simpler browser game (knife only, elevator as exit), so treat
  them with extra caution.

## Gaps I need from your play (screenshots welcome)
1. **Getting seen:** does it cost health or fail the level? Do guards shoot? Is there a "?" suspicious phase?
2. **Level structure:** several floors joined by elevators? Is the between-floor upgrade pick (weapon/item/boost)
   real, and does it reset each level?
3. **Guard types:** any visibly different guards (armored, heavy, flashlight, dogs, cameras)?
4. **Bosses:** how does a boss fight work? Entourage? Does it take several hits? Any special rule?
5. **Sniper levels:** viewpoint and controls, and what counts as failure.
6. **Heists:** what the "brainteasers" are (safe dial, lasers, timing?) and whether kills are allowed.
7. **Lasers/lights:** what happens when you touch them.
8. **Weapons:** do they change how you play (ranged throw, gun, silencer) or only how kills look?
9. **Characters:** what levelling improves.
10. **Map:** is there a chapter/world map, and a boss every N levels?
